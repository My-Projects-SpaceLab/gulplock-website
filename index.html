FAQ

Frequently asked.
Answered straight.

Can I cancel it once the sound starts?+
No. Once the sound is playing there is no cancel button — Free or Pro. You drink, the camera confirms, it stops. That’s the only way out. Before it starts you can still change your schedule.
What’s a postpone, exactly?+
Once the sound is playing, tap the screen 3 times to reveal the Postpone button. It delays the next check by 30 minutes. Use it when you’re in a meeting, driving, or need a few more minutes. Free includes 2 postpones per day. It only appears on the verification screen after the sound has started.
Can I skip a whole day?+
On Free, no — the schedule runs every day once you’ve set it. Pro lets you skip one full day when you genuinely need to, without turning the whole app off.
Is this a hydration tracker?+
No. We don’t measure millilitres and we’re not a streak app. There’s one job here: make it slightly harder to skip drinking than to just drink.
Can it be faked?+
Yes. So can every habit app ever made. Faking it only costs you the thing you downloaded this to fix. We’d rather tell you that upfront than pretend otherwise.
Does the camera upload anything?+
No. Verification runs on-device. Frames are processed locally. Verified photos are saved only on your device. Everyone gets a proof gallery, and Pro keeps a longer history. Nothing is uploaded to the cloud.
