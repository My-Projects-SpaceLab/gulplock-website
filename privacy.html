Privacy Policy

GulpLock · Effective date: October 20, 2026
Service Provider: Akshay Varala · Contact: support.gulplock@gmail.com

This privacy policy applies to the GulpLock mobile application and any related services operated by Akshay Varala (the “Application”, “we”, “us”).

GulpLock is a reminder and commitment app. It schedules a water-drop sound that continues until you complete an on-device camera verification. It is not a hydration tracker, health monitor, or medical device. It does not measure water volume, keep drinking streaks, or build a health record.

1. Information we collect

No account required. You can use the free version without providing a name, email, or phone number.

1.1 Information stored on your device

The Application may store the following locally on your device:

  • Schedule settings (start time, end time, interval)
  • App preferences and onboarding state
  • Postpone / skip usage counts (to enforce Free limits)
  • Proof photos: a still image saved after each successful verification (see Section 1.2)

This information stays on your device unless a feature below says otherwise.

1.2 Camera and on-device processing

When a check is active, GulpLock may use the device camera to recognize visual information relevant to the requested action, such as:

  • Objects (for example a bottle or glass)
  • Face, hand, or wrist position
  • Actions associated with holding or drinking from a container

GulpLock uses on-device machine-learning and computer-vision models for object detection, face detection, hand/wrist positioning, and recognition of actions such as holding or drinking from a container. Processing occurs locally on the device. Camera frames are not uploaded to GulpLock, Firebase, RevenueCat, or any cloud-based AI service.

Camera frames are analyzed in memory for the verification step only.

When a verification succeeds, a still image is saved only on your device and appears in your proof gallery. This applies to all users. Free keeps up to 45 active days and Pro keeps up to 90, and older photos are removed automatically. Older days are blurred for Free users. Pro users can delete individual photos inside the app. Anyone can delete all photos by clearing the app's data or uninstalling it. We never receive these images.

No hydration history. Camera verification is used only to stop the current reminder. GulpLock does not use it to create a drinking log, measure water intake, track streaks, or build a personal health profile.

1.3 Alarms and reminders

GulpLock schedules alarms and reminder sounds through Android system services so the sound can start at the times you set and continue until you complete verification (or use an allowed postpone). These reminders are application functionality, not medical alerts. They do not require location access.

  • Notifications and full-screen screen: while the sound plays, a persistent notification is shown. When your phone is locked, the verification screen may appear full screen. You can leave it with the Home or Back button.
  • Background and restart: a foreground service keeps the sound playing, and alarms are rescheduled after your phone restarts. The app may ask to be excluded from battery optimization so alarms are on time.
  • Activity recognition: GulpLock uses Android's activity recognition only to detect when you are in a vehicle or on a bicycle, so reminders can pause while you travel. This is evaluated on your device. The detected activity is not stored, shown as health or fitness data, or sent off your device.

1.4 Automatically collected technical data

When you use the Application, certain technical information may be collected through third-party services we use (see Section 3), for example:

  • Device type, OS version, and app version
  • Crash logs and diagnostic data
  • Anonymous or pseudonymous usage events (e.g. screens opened, feature used)
  • Purchase / subscription entitlement status
  • Device identifiers used by Firebase and RevenueCat, such as an app instance ID and, where available, the Android advertising ID. We use them for analytics and subscription management, not to show ads

We do not use this data to identify you personally beyond what is needed for stability, analytics, and subscription management.

2. How we use information

  • To run your schedule and play the reminder sound
  • To perform on-device camera verification so the sound can stop
  • To store Pro proof images locally if you enable that feature
  • To diagnose crashes and improve app stability (Crashlytics)
  • To understand aggregate usage and improve the product (Analytics)
  • To manage and verify Free vs Pro entitlements (RevenueCat / store)
  • To respond if you email support

We do not sell your personal information. We do not use camera frames for advertising.

3. Third-party services

GulpLock uses the following third-party services. Each has its own privacy policy:

  • Google Play Services — platform and billing support
  • Google Analytics for Firebase — app usage analytics
  • Firebase Crashlytics — crash and error reporting
  • RevenueCat — purchase and subscription entitlement management

These providers may process the technical and purchase-related data described above according to their policies. Camera frames used for verification are not sent to these services.

4. Purchases

Subscriptions and lifetime unlocks are processed by Google Play (and, if applicable, the App Store). RevenueCat may process purchase and subscription-related information needed to manage and verify Pro entitlements. Payment credentials are handled by Google Play or the App Store and are not provided to GulpLock.

5. When we may share information

We may disclose information only:

  • As required by law (e.g. subpoena or similar process)
  • When we believe in good faith it is necessary to protect rights, safety, or investigate fraud
  • With service providers who process data on our behalf under appropriate agreements (e.g. Firebase, RevenueCat)

6. Your rights and how to delete your data

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, or restrict processing of personal data, or to withdraw consent.

How to delete data related to GulpLock:

  • Local schedule & settings: Uninstall the app, or clear the app’s data in system settings.
  • Proof gallery photos: Delete them inside the app (Pro), or uninstall the app.
  • Camera access: Revoke permission in system settings. Verification will stop working.
  • Analytics / crash data held by us or our providers: Email support.gulplock@gmail.com with the subject "GulpLock data deletion request". We will delete or anonymize the data we can link to you within 30 days, except where the law requires us to keep it.
  • Subscriptions: Cancel in Google Play (or App Store) account settings. Refunds follow the store’s policies.

California residents (CCPA/CPRA): You may request to know, delete, or opt out of sale/sharing of personal information. We do not sell personal information. Contact us at the email above to exercise these rights.

7. Data retention

  • On-device data (settings, gallery): remains until you delete it or uninstall the app.
  • Crash and analytics data: retained according to the applicable retention settings and policies of the respective Firebase services.
  • Support emails: retained as long as needed to resolve your request, then deleted or archived as appropriate.

8. Children

The Application is not directed at children under 16 (or the higher age required in your region). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided data, contact us and we will delete it.

9. Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures appropriate to the nature of the data we process. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure. On-device camera processing reduces exposure by keeping frames off our servers.

Third-party providers (Google, RevenueCat) may process data in countries other than your own. Where required, they use appropriate safeguards (e.g. Standard Contractual Clauses). Camera verification data does not leave your device.
